# Movies: design (agreed in chat, 2026-11-28)

Intent: students watch English films together on Ufq, with short SAT breaks, room chat and a word list.
Source: Wikimedia Commons only (user rejected YouTube; pirate sources ruled out). Live search is limited to that library.
Rooms: public, or private (code, or friend/invite). Host controls playback. Up to 10.
SAT breaks: min(6, max(1, minutes/15)), equally spaced. 1 hard; miss -> 2 medium; miss -> 3 easy; finished players spectate; film resumes when all are done (12 min cap).
Chat: text and voice only, over the film. Subtitles from Commons TimedText (not every film has them).
Code: public/dc/movies.js, movies.css, supabase/migrations/20261208_movies.sql.
